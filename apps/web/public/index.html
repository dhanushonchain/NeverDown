The product

Two planes.
One bounded playbook.

A Resilience Contract defines what the system may do. Recovery and procurement then work at different speeds, with different responsibilities.

01 / Fast Recovery Plane

Keep the application moving.

Health checks detect an unhealthy dependency and the gateway switches to a preapproved, healthy standby. This path does not depend on an LLM or a payment.

Deterministic · preapproved · independent
02 / Procurement Plane

Replenish what was used.

The agent discovers replacement capability, verifies the provider and asks policy to authorize a bounded purchase before installation.

Discover · verify · authorize · procure
How it works

Three steps. Clear boundaries.

01 — Detect & recover

Restore first.

NeverDown detects an unhealthy dependency and switches to a preapproved standby when one is available.

02 — Discover & verify

Check the replacement.

Arkiv surfaces capability offers, ENS resolves provider identity, and Proof-of-Capability tests the candidate service.

03 — Procure & prove

Let policy decide.

PolicyEngine authorizes or denies. Hedera x402 handles an approved payment; HCS records decision evidence.
