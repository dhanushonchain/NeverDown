The operating model

One policy.
Two independent clocks.

01 / Fast recovery

Keep serving.

A deterministic gateway checks health and switches to a preapproved standby. It works while the agent, discovery network, or payment rail is unavailable.

Health → switch → serve
02 / Controlled procurement

Restore redundancy.

The Supreme Agent looks for a replacement, verifies the seller and capability, and asks the Resilience Contract to authorize any purchase and installation.

Discover → verify → authorize → install
Under the Resilience Contract
